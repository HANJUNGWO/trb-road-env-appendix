Appendix B. Human Survey Instrument

This appendix provides the survey instrument used for human validation.

Survey items

Participants were asked to evaluate each image using the following five items:

1. The scene feels rich in greenery.
2. The view feels wide and open.
3. The scene appears visually complex.
4. The scene feels safe for driving.
5. The scene appears likely to cause low driving stress.

Response scale

All items were rated on a 7-point Likert scale:

1 = Strongly disagree
2 = Disagree
3 = Slightly disagree
4 = Neutral
5 = Slightly agree
6 = Agree
7 = Strongly agree

Survey instruction

Please evaluate each road scene based only on what is directly visible in the image.

Respond according to your immediate visual impression as a driver.

There are no correct or incorrect answers.
